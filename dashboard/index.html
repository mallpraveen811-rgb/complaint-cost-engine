How this was built, and definitions
Data
Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset (Kaggle): 9 linked tables loaded into PostgreSQL, cleaned, and modelled as a star schema.
AI step
An LLM (Claude) read a random sample of written Portuguese reviews and gave each one complaint type (from a fixed list of 12), a severity, a sentiment and a "won't buy again" flag.
Complaint order
An order whose review the AI tagged with a complaint type.
Revenue at risk
Value of complaint orders where the problem is high-severity or the customer says they won't buy again. It is an estimate of exposure, not a measured loss.
Scaling
Sample totals are multiplied by written reviews ÷ tagged reviews to estimate the total for all written reviews. With roughly 10–90 complaints per type in the sample, treat the ranking as more reliable than the exact amounts.
Late
Delivered after the date the store promised at purchase.
Summary
Written by the LLM from that month's KPI numbers only; a script checked that every number in it appears in the data.
