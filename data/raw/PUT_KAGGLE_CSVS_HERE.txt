Download "Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist" from Kaggle and unzip the 9 CSV files here:
olist_customers_dataset.csv, olist_geolocation_dataset.csv, olist_order_items_dataset.csv,
olist_order_payments_dataset.csv, olist_order_reviews_dataset.csv, olist_orders_dataset.csv,
olist_products_dataset.csv, olist_sellers_dataset.csv, product_category_name_translation.csv

To test without it: python tests/make_sample_data.py creates synthetic look-alike files here.
